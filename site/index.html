Free, open-source Spotify now-playing software for Linux and Raspberry Pi. Show album artwork, animated vinyl, track information and a live audio spectrum.

A Spotify display for your own setup

Album art and spinning vinyl

See cover artwork, song titles and artists on a full-screen music display, with animated record and sleeve options.

Live audio visualizer

Watch the audio spectrum react to your music while controlling playback from the Spotify app on your phone or computer.

Linux PC or Raspberry Pi

Use compatible x86_64, ARM64 or ARMv7 Linux hardware with Spotify Soloist. Spotify Premium is required.
